Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 5, 2026 | Last Updated: November 16, 2026

Welcome to QuickSend. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy details how we handle information in connection with the QuickSend mobile application (the "App") designed for Android devices.

1. Information We Collect

Our core philosophy is absolute privacy. We believe that your personal messaging data belongs solely to you.

✨ No External Data Collection: QuickSend does not collect, capture, transmit, track, or store any personal data, phone numbers, contact details, or message content on any external servers or third-party cloud services.

  • No Tracking & Analytics: We do not integrate third-party tracking, advertising SDKs, or user profiling analytics.
  • No Account Required: You do not need to register, sign in, or provide any personal credentials to use QuickSend.

2. Local Storage & Data Management

To enhance your user experience, QuickSend provides an optional recent history feature for previously entered phone numbers. Please note:

  • All history details (phone numbers, optional draft text, and timestamps) are stored strictly locally in the App's secure private database on your device.
  • This data never leaves your device and is never uploaded, synced, or backed up to any external server by QuickSend.
  • User Control & Deletion: You have full control over your data. You can erase all search history at any time using the "Clear History" button in Settings, or by clearing the App data via Android Settings > Apps > QuickSend > Storage > Clear Data. Uninstalling the App permanently removes all stored data.

3. Third-Party Integrations (WhatsApp)

QuickSend acts as a lightweight utility that opens standard Android intents using official WhatsApp deep links (e.g., https://api.whatsapp.com/send or https://wa.me/).

  • When you tap "Open in WhatsApp", you are redirected to the official WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business application installed on your device.
  • Any interaction, conversation, or data transfer occurring within WhatsApp is governed exclusively by the WhatsApp Privacy Policy. We do not have access to, nor do we monitor, intercept, or store your chats or messages.
  • Disclaimer: QuickSend is an independent utility and is not affiliated with, sponsored, authorized, or endorsed by WhatsApp LLC or Meta Platforms, Inc.

4. Device Permissions

QuickSend requires minimal permissions strictly necessary for its core functionality:

  • Internet Permission (android.permission.INTERNET): Used solely to check network availability and dispatch official deep link intents to open the messaging platform.
  • Package Visibility: QuickSend checks for installed WhatsApp packages (com.whatsapp, com.whatsapp.w4b) to seamlessly route link intents to the appropriate application.
  • Zero Invasive Permissions: QuickSend does not request access to your Contacts (READ_CONTACTS), Call Logs, Camera, Microphone, Location, or External Files.

5. Data Security

Because all history information resides locally on your device, it is secured by the standard Android application sandbox environment. No data is ever transmitted across the internet to our servers, eliminating external breach risks.

6. Children's Privacy

QuickSend does not address anyone under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). Because we do not collect any personal data, our App is safe for users of all ages, including children. We do not knowingly solicit or collect data from minors.

7. Changes to this Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically for any modifications.
